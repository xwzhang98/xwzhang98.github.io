Research Experience

  • 2021 to present
    Graduate Research Assistant
    McWilliams Center for Cosmology & Astrophysics, Carnegie Mellon University
    • Super-resolution of N-body simulations across cosmic time: 512× more particles, validated on power spectra, halo mass functions, and merger trees. First author, MNRAS.
    • Deterministic emulator that recovers the specific realization implied by a simulation’s initial conditions (first author, OJAp); the same method, applied by a mentee to the Lyman-α forest, reaches sub-percent accuracy at 260× the speed of the hydrodynamic solver (second author, arXiv:2507.16189).
    • Cosmology-aware super-resolution: one model for every cosmology in the Quijote suite. First author, submitted.
    • SimAgents, a multi-agent LLM system that configures and runs MP-GADGET simulations from the literature: 80% fewer setup errors, 83× faster setup. First author, IJCNLP-AACL 2025.
  • 2020 to 2021
    Research Assistant
    Institute of Mathematical Sciences, ShanghaiTech University
    • Topological data analysis of high-dimensional cosmological datasets; tropical-geometry representations of neural network decision boundaries.

Open Source Software

  • CosmoSR
    • Generative adversarial network that super-resolves Quijote simulations at multiple cosmologies; used in the cosmology-aware super-resolution paper (submitted).
  • SimAgents
    • Multi-agent LLM framework that turns a physical setup described in the literature into a runnable MP-GADGET configuration and first-look analysis, with a validated database of simulation parameters from published suites for evaluation.
  • SREmulator
    • Neural models that transform stochastic super-resolution outputs into deterministic realizations matching specific initial conditions.
  • map2map (stylegan2)
    • Style-conditioned super-resolution models for N-body simulations (512× mass resolution), developed on Yin Li’s map2map framework; upstream PR merged, 2021.
