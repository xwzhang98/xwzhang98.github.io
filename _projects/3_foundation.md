There is no common language for comparing astrophysical simulations: summary statistics are picked by hand, models are trained per code, and no principled measure says how similar two simulations are. My current first-author project builds that language — a **shared latent space** ingesting heterogeneous simulation outputs (3D fields, projected maps, spectra, halo catalogs) across codes and resolutions, designed to be **invariant to initial-condition realizations** while staying sensitive to cosmology.

In parallel, I am studying the **physics interpretability of [AION-1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17960)** — an omnimodal foundation model for astronomical observations — with the CMU Machine Learning Department: foundation models for astronomy are arriving faster than our understanding of what their representations actually encode.
